# Before enabling the courier-interest database

This is a review checklist and proposed notice, not a certification of GDPR compliance.

The immediate recruitment link uses the applicant's own email app and the operator's contact address. It does not save applications into the Fair Delivery database.

Before enabling COURIER_INTEREST_OPEN:
- Confirm the controller's official business identity and contact.
- Confirm a durable database and the hosting/processing locations, processor terms, access controls, transfers and applicable safeguards.
- Review the actual retention and backup limits. The live registry currently removes entries after 90 days; verify any residual copies and ensure inbox messages are handled according to a documented retention policy.
- Confirm procedures for withdrawal of consent, access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and complaints.
- Set the reviewed notice and contact as private deployment configuration. Keep the detailed qualification route closed before its separate compliance review.
- Test with synthetic applicants, not third-party personal data.

Proposed notice (adapt and approve before collecting):
The early-interest form collects email and optional region, with explicit consent, only to contact the person about possible independent courier cooperation. It does not create a courier account, guarantee work, or constitute an automated eligibility decision. The operator and authorized hosting processor have access as necessary. Identify actual data recipients, processing locations and valid transfer safeguards; the current hosting region is Oregon, USA. Render publishes its Data Processing Addendum at https://render.com/dpa; applicability must be confirmed by the operator. The proposed live-registry retention is at most 90 days, unless the candidate separately proceeds to a new recruitment stage under a reviewed policy. State actual backup retention. Consent is optional and can be withdrawn without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal. State the controller identity, contact and rights, and the right to complain to https://www.dpa.gr/. No application is required to view the public recruitment page.
