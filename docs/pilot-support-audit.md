# Pilot support and assignment transparency
- Authenticated customers can report a problem only on their own order, with structured categories and no additional personal details. Guest orders are not eligible yet.
- Admin reviews tickets manually; refund review is not a payment, refund approval, accounting adjustment or automated decision. No money changes hands.
- Staff with existing order access can read new operationalHistory and operationalTiming fields. Times measure ready/assigned to pickup, not actual rider waiting or payroll. Legacy orders have no invented event history.
- Keep existing data, rates and pilot flags. No new external service or paid plan.
- Before commercial launch: review retention, privacy notice, customer contact options, dispute process, real payment provider, legal obligations and measurement of rider arrival/waiting. The dashboard lists up to 200 tickets; add pagination before scaling.
